Physical geography studies the natural features and phenomena of planet Earth. Mount Everest, located in the Mahalangur Himal sub-range of the Himalayas along the border of Nepal and China, is Earth's highest mountain above sea level, reaching an officially recognized elevation of 8,848.86 meters. The Nile River in northeastern Africa is traditionally regarded as the longest river system on Earth, measuring approximately 6,650 kilometers in length and flowing through eleven African countries before discharging into the Mediterranean Sea. The Amazon River in South America possesses the largest drainage basin and the greatest average water discharge volume of any river system. The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest of Earth's oceanic divisions, covering an area exceeding 165 million square kilometers and containing the Mariana Trench, whose deepest point, Challenger Deep, descends approximately 10,928 meters below sea level. The Sahara Desert in Northern Africa is the largest hot desert on Earth, encompassing over 9.2 million square kilometers across twelve countries.
